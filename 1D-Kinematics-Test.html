1-D Kinematics Unit Test

Formula sheet

Speed and velocity

vav = d ÷ Δt  (speed, no direction)
vav = Δd ÷ Δt  (velocity, with direction)
Δd = d2 − d1

Acceleration

a = (v2 − v1) ÷ Δt

The 5 equations (constant acceleration)

1. v2 = v1 + aΔt
2. Δd = ½(v2 + v1)Δt
3. Δd = v1Δt + ½aΔt²
4. Δd = v2Δt − ½aΔt²
5. v2² = v1² + 2aΔd

Graphs

Slope of position–time graph = velocity
Slope of velocity–time graph = acceleration
Area under velocity–time graph = displacement
Rectangle: A = l × w  ·  Triangle: A = ½ b × h

Constants and conversions

g = 9.8 m/s² [down]
km/h → m/s: × 1000 m/km ÷ 3600 s/h
“From rest” → v1 = 0  ·  “To a stop” → v2 = 0
